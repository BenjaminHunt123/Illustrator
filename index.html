Production Workflow Pipeline

The structured 6-stage lifecycle for editorial commissions, book covers, and commercial key art.

01

Client Brief & Visual Discovery

Reviewing project objectives, audience demographics, aspect ratios, color palettes, reference moodboards, and usage license scopes.

Moodboard & Scope Brief
02

Thumbnailing & Composition

Generating 3 to 5 rapid, low-resolution rough thumbnail sketches exploring composition, perspective, focal points, and silhouette readability.

3-5 Rough Sketch Options
03

Line Art & Color Rough Approval

Cleaning vector line art or pencil pass and applying a flat color key preview to establish mood, lighting direction, and client approval.

Clean Lines & Color Key
04

High-Detail Painting & Texturing

Rendering full textures, highlights, ambient occlusion, depth blur, custom brushwork, and intricate background elements.

Rendering & Textures Pass
05

Color Grading & CMYK/RGB Export

Fine-tuning adjustment layers, contrast levels, sharpening, and exporting high-res CMYK (TIF/PDF) for print and sRGB (PNG) for web.

CMYK Print & RGB Export
06

Client Licensing & Asset Delivery

Delivering layered PSD/AI master files, final license agreement paperwork, invoice release, and archiving master assets.

Master Files & Licensing
